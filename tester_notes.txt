New Mechanic: Traitor Tester

When enabled, a specified number of "Traitor Tester Parts" will appear randomly around the map. The innocents should work to find all parts, and bring them to a central location. When they do: a random traitor is revealed to the entire server!

Be careful, traitors can see where every part is, and might be inclined to make sure innocent players don't find them. Carrying a part occupies your main weapon slot, leaving you even more vulnerable while you have one. If you're thinking about testing traitors by having them pick up a part, there's nothing stopping traitors from picking up the parts themselves!

Traitors... you might be thinking, "What's stopping me from just picking up all the parts and hiding them, or holding onto them all round?" Well, a few things. First, when you pick up a part, it instantly drains a credit. The longer you hold onto it, the more credits you'll lose (dropping doesn't reset the timer). Don't have any credits? That's okay, if you're still holding onto a traitor tester part, you'll start losing your equipment! Okay, fine, "I'll just destroy the part," you say. That's bad too, if you destroy a part, it just lowers the number of parts needed to activate the test. So, really, you should just try to prevent innocents from finding them.

A final note for innocents: if you destroy a part, it ruins the whole thing. Just be good and take it to your friends.
