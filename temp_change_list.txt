== Role Variants ==
A new system of role variants: specialized versions of the base roles, off by default (ttt_role_variants convar).

- Kingpin (traitor):     Funds the traitor team from a distance. No traitor chat, and never learns who his fellow traitors are -- but they all know who he is. Starts richer and earns credits whenever the traitor side gets a kill.
- Spy (innocent):        Appears as a traitor to the traitor team on the scoreboard, radar, and target ID. Jams traitor chat and limits every traitor to 1 credit while alive. No innocent is ever told he exists.
- Deputy (detective):    A secret detective. Full detective shop and credits, but nobody -- not even another detective -- is ever told he's one.
- VIP (innocent):        Every innocent is told who he is. Traitors are limited to 1 credit while he's alive, but he personally takes more damage, and if he dies, every innocent takes more damage for the rest of the round.
- Rogue (neutral):       A third faction. Appears as an innocent to everyone, shops from the traitor list, and wins alone by being the last terrorist standing.
- Sleeper Agent (neutral): Starts as an innocent nobody suspects. If every traitor dies while he's still alive, he secretly becomes a real traitor with fresh credits and bonus round time -- the server is told someone has woken up, but never who.

Each variant's odds of appearing, minimum/maximum count, and other tuning are all server convars.

== New Weapon ==
- Cluster Grenade (traitor):  A grenade that splits into four smaller grenades on detonation, which then go off in a second wave shortly after.

== New Mechanic: Traitor Tester ==
Traitor Tester Parts spawn around the map. Innocents who gather enough of them together reveal a random traitor to the whole server. Traitors can see every part's location and can pick parts up themselves to stall or hide them, at the cost of steadily draining their own credits (or equipment, once they're out) the longer they hold one.

== Other Additions ==
- Hopper Mine:                A throwable proximity mine.
- Victory/defeat sounds at the end of a round.
- A dedicated Role info tab in the F1 menu explaining every role and variant, including the reasoning behind how to play them.

== Changes ==
- Death messages now name the killer's specific role variant (eg. "they were a Kingpin!") instead of just their base role.
- Round-end screen and body-search results now show role variants (eg. "Traitor - Kingpin").
- Various notification adjustments.
